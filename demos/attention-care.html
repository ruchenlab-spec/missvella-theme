Dress care

How to unpack your gown, get it ready to wear and put it away for next time.

Find my fabric↓

Care steps

Start with the label sewn into your dress. It accounts for the lining, trims and finish as well as the outer fabric.

When your dress arrives

Your dress may arrive inside out to protect the surface. Unfold it on a clean bed, support the skirt with both hands and gently turn it right side out.

Creases before the occasion

Unpack early so the skirt has room to settle. Check the care label before using an iron or steamer. If heat instructions are missing, send us the style before treating a crease.

Spills & cleaning

Leave stain removers and rubbing brushes aside until you’ve checked the label. If it specifies professional spot cleaning, show the cleaner the mark and let them know what caused it.

Putting your dress away

Store the dress clean and dry, away from direct sunlight. For a gown suited to hanging, use its inner hanger loops. Store heavily beaded styles flat with the weight of the skirt supported.

Fabric notes

Find the fabric named on your product page for a closer look at how to handle it.

Satin

Put rings and bracelets on after dressing. When you pick up a satin train, lift it from underneath so a clasp or rough nail cannot catch the smooth face.

Chiffon

Lift the skirt from beneath the lining when you climb stairs. Let the sheer layers fall back into place one at a time so their edges stay free.

Tulle

Separate the net layers gently with open hands. If two tiers catch, lay the skirt down and ease the threads apart before lifting it again.

Mesh

Keep hooks and exposed zip teeth away from the open net. Support mesh panels with your palm as you dress so your fingers do not pull through an opening.

Lace

Fasten jewellery after your dress is on. If a clasp catches a lace motif, support the lace and open the clasp before easing it free.

Crepe

Give a crepe skirt room to fall to its full length. If it has a lace bodice or embellished waist, check the label for the whole dress before treating a crease.

Velvet

Leave space around a velvet bodice in the wardrobe. Hanger clips and the weight of other clothes can leave impressions in the raised pile.

Sequin Fabric

Support the weight of the dress as you lift it. Store a heavily embellished gown flat, with a clean, smooth cloth between folds to keep the sequins from catching the lining.
