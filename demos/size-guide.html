How to measure

Read measurement instructions
Bust
Measure around the fullest part of your bust, keeping the tape level around your back.
Waist
Measure around the narrowest part of your natural waistline without pulling the tape tight.
Hips
Measure around the fullest part of your hips, keeping the tape parallel to the floor.
Hollow to floor
Stand barefoot and measure vertically from the hollow between your collarbones to the floor.
Height
Stand straight and barefoot. Measure from the top of your head to the floor.

Take measurements over underwear. For best results, have someone help you measure.
