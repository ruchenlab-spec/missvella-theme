3. Estimated Total Delivery Time

Standard Shipping. Add 2–5 business days for order processing to the transit time for your destination in Section 2. For example, a US order typically takes 9–17 business days from payment confirmation to delivery, including 7–12 business days in transit.

Express Shipping. Allow 5–10 business days in total, including 2–5 business days for processing and 3–5 business days in transit.

Delivery dates are estimates. Customs clearance, carrier operations, weather, public holidays, incorrect address information and other shipping disruptions can affect when your parcel arrives.

If you are ordering for a wedding, prom or another event, allow extra time for fitting and alterations. For urgent orders, contact hello@missvella.com before purchasing to discuss shipping options.

We will comply with applicable consumer laws regarding shipping delays, revised shipment dates, cancellation rights, and refunds.
