Planning for a special occasion? To estimate when your MISSVELLA order will arrive, add the time we need to make and prepare your items to the shipping time for your destination. You can find shipping fees, transit estimates and tracking information below.

Order processing time

We process orders Monday through Saturday. Once your order is confirmed and payment is received, allow 2–5 business days for processing, including production. During this time, we verify your order, make your items, check their quality and pack them for shipment.

Ordering for a wedding, prom or another event? Allow time for delivery, fitting and any alterations. If your date is close, email hello@missvella.com before ordering with your event date and delivery location. We will check the available options and do our best to help you meet your date.

Order confirmation and tracking

After you place your order, we will email your order confirmation. Please check your items, sizes, colors, shipping address and contact details. If you spot an error, email us promptly with your order number and the correction. We may be able to update the address before dispatch.

When your order ships, we will email a shipping confirmation with the available tracking details. You can follow your parcel on our Track your order page. The first tracking update may take some time to appear after the carrier receives your parcel.

If your order is delayed

Before dispatch. If we expect processing to take longer than estimated, we will contact you with a revised dispatch estimate and explain your options, including cancellation and a refund where applicable.

After dispatch. If your parcel has not arrived by the end of the estimated transit window for your shipping method and destination, or if tracking has stopped updating, email hello@missvella.com with your order number and, if available, your tracking number. We will check with the carrier and let you know what happens next.

Parcels returned because of an address error

If tracking shows that your parcel is being returned because the address was incorrect or incomplete, email hello@missvella.com with your order number and full corrected address, including the postal code and any apartment or unit number. Once the parcel is back with us, we will confirm the available shipping options and any applicable reshipping fee with you before arranging a new shipment.

Lost, damaged or missing packages

If your parcel appears lost, arrives damaged or is marked as delivered but you cannot find it, email hello@missvella.com with the following details.

  • Your order number and, if available, your tracking number
  • A brief description of what happened
  • Photos of the damage if the parcel or an item arrived damaged

You can choose a replacement shipment or a refund. Tell us your preference when you email us and we will arrange it for you.
