Shipping & Delivery

10 questions
When will my order be shipped?

Standard order processing takes 2–5 business days after your order is confirmed and payment is received. This covers preparation, checks, and packing. Carrier transit starts after dispatch and is additional to processing time. Business days exclude weekends and public holidays.

审核说明：运输政策 §1。删除旧 FAQ 的全店手工、10–15 工作日及 15–30 天制作承诺。
依据：Missvella Shipping Policy
What shipping options and prices do you offer?

For destinations supported at checkout, our published shipping options are:

  • Standard: $15 USD, with estimated transit of 4–10 business days after dispatch.
  • Express: $30 USD, with estimated transit of 3–5 business days after dispatch.

Processing time is additional. Available options and any destination-specific differences are shown at checkout. See our Shipping Policy.

审核说明：运输政策 §2。USD 为费率单位，不据此承诺全店只能用美元结算。
依据：Missvella Shipping Policy
How long will it take to receive my order?

Total delivery time includes processing plus carrier transit:

  • Standard: approximately 6–15 business days in total.
  • Express: approximately 5–10 business days in total.

These are estimates, not guaranteed arrival dates. Customs, holidays, weather, and carrier disruptions can affect delivery. Allow extra time before an event for fitting and any alterations. Read the Shipping Policy for details.

审核说明：运输政策 §3；2–5 + 4–10 = 6–15，2–5 + 3–5 = 5–10。
依据：Missvella Shipping Policy
How do I qualify for free shipping?

Free Standard Shipping applies when your merchandise subtotal is $180 USD or more after discounts and before shipping charges, for supported destinations. It is applied automatically when the threshold is met. Express Shipping remains $30 USD unless a specific promotion says otherwise. Review the final shipping charge at checkout.

审核说明：运输政策 §2；不能简化成任何订单总额满180美元或免加急运费。
依据：Missvella Shipping Policy

International deliveries may attract customs duties, import taxes, or other local charges. Unless checkout states otherwise, these are not included in your order total and are your responsibility. Any charges collected by Missvella at checkout will be disclosed there. Customs clearance may also affect delivery time; your local customs authority can advise on potential charges.

Yes, you may request a return for an eligible item within 15 calendar days of delivery, including for fit, color, style preference, or a change of mind. What condition must my returned item be in?

For a standard return, the item must be unworn, unwashed, unaltered, and in its original condition, with all original and security tags intact. It must be free from stains, makeup, perfume, odors, pet hair, and other signs of wear. Return any included accessories or detachable parts. You may carefully try it on, but it must not have been worn for an event or photoshoot. Different rights apply to faulty or incorrectly supplied goods.

审核说明：退货政策 §2；不能移除安全标签后再承诺普通退货。
